Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Saans and our recommendations.

Are Open-Meteo readings the same as nearby ground monitors?

No. Open-Meteo produces atmospheric model estimates that combine satellite observations with meteorological simulations. They provide an accurate general picture across a city, but local ground monitors can measure higher concentrations near traffic or localized dust.

What is diurnal thermal inversion?

During winter nights and early mornings, cold air stays trapped near the ground beneath a layer of warm air, holding smoke and emissions close to breathing height. Midday solar heating breaks this inversion, allowing cleaner dispersion between 11:00 and 16:00.

Does the AI ever change numbers or medical rules?

No. All calculations are performed deterministically in Python before any AI is called. The language model (powered by {{ ai_name }}) is only asked to synthesize explanations, and a strict numeric verification filter drops any ungrounded figures from the response.
